What the GeoAI does

K-Means groups observed cells into recurring connectivity-community profiles. It is descriptive decision support; the priority score remains transparent and separate from the AI model.

How to use the map

Choose a priority lens, search a locality or grid, and click a cell. The first screen shows only the key decision information. Expand the lower sections for network evidence, community context, model details, or the what-if simulator.

Responsible-use guardrails

  • Priority is relative within Kuala Selangor, not a regulatory service classification.
  • No Ookla observation is treated as an Evidence Gap, not as proof of good or poor connectivity.
  • School and healthcare counts are OpenStreetMap-mapped facilities, not official administrative totals.
  • What-if scenarios describe hypothetical connectivity outcomes, not tower placement or RF propagation predictions.
  • GeoAI clusters are descriptive profiles, not ground-truth labels.
